BowerBite: grab-finding for post-production

Version 1.4 · Windows and Mac · 7-day free trial

Curate like a bowerbird. Collect the best bites from every transcript and build something beautiful.

BowerBite (formerly SEEK) turns your interview transcripts into something you can actually search: by word, by phrase and by meaning. It's built by a reality TV editor for editors, assistant editors and story producers who are tired of scrolling through transcripts hoping to strike gold.

Your transcripts and media never leave your machine. No cloud processing and no generative AI. Everything, including transcription, runs on your own computer.

Website: bowerbite.com

What it does
SEARCH: fast keyword search across every transcript at once, with timecodes. Filter by speaker or source. Click a timecode to copy it.
SCOUT: search by meaning. Ask for "Billy feeling anxious" and it finds him saying "I'm still incredibly nervous", even though he never said "anxious". Search by mood, tone or topic across every contestant.
STITCH: the Frankengrab builder. Type the line you need and BowerBite assembles it from words the person actually said, picking the most natural-sounding joins. ENHANCED mode with Alternatives suggests similar words they did say when the exact word isn't there.
STASH: collect your best grabs under headings, then send them to your edit as markers (Avid, Premiere, Final Cut or Resolve), or save them as a document for your producer.
New in 1.4
Real Time Transcribe: drop in video or audio and watch the transcript appear live in MARKERS, with correct timecode. It works in around 100 languages (auto-detected) and separates speakers. Choose Fast, Balanced or Best accuracy. Rename a speaker once and the whole transcript updates.
Translate: translate a foreign-language transcript to English line by line, keeping every timecode. Switch between the original and English at any time. SEARCH and SCOUT follow whichever you're viewing.
Renamable sources: give any source a friendly name without touching the file on disk.
Premiere Pro transcript import (.txt, .csv, .srt).
Installing

Download the installer for your computer from bowerbite.com or the Releases page.

Windows

You'll need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 1GB of free disk space (more if you download the larger transcription models), and 8GB of RAM or more recommended.

Download BowerBite_Setup.exe and run it.
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". That's because BowerBite isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.
BowerBite installs just for you, so you don't need an admin password. Open it from the Start menu.
Mac

You'll need: macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer.

Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4…): download BowerBite_Mac.dmg
Intel Macs: download BowerBite_Mac_Intel.dmg

Not sure which you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says "Chip: Apple M…", you're on Apple Silicon.

Open the DMG and drag BowerBite into Applications.

Internet use

The search language pack is included, so SEARCH, SCOUT and STITCH work offline. BowerBite goes online only to:

download a transcription or translation model the first time you choose it
check your subscription (it keeps working offline for up to 14 days between checks)
Getting started
Add your transcripts. Drag them into the SOURCES panel on the left, or click Browse…. BowerBite reads:
Interview transcripts (.docx)
Timecoded MIV transcripts (.pdf or .docx)
SubCap exports (.txt), subtitles (.srt, .vtt), marker spreadsheets (.csv)
Premiere Pro transcript exports
Or transcribe video and audio directly with Real Time Transcribe
Pick a tab on the right and start searching.
Click a source badge on any result to copy the source name, ready to paste into your edit. MATCH SOURCE narrows your search to that one source.
Stash the grabs you want, then use Send to NLE in the STASH tab.

Getting markers into your edit: in the STASH tab, choose Send to NLE and pick your system: Avid Marker Text, FCPXML (Premiere / Final Cut) or EDL (Resolve). Then import that file using your editing software's marker or XML/EDL import.

SCOUT tips
Describe the grab in plain words: "someone nervous before judging", "jen feeling happy".
Name a person to focus on them.
Use the Tone menu to find grabs by attitude: competitive, confident, emotional, nervous, angry.
Add "not about the judges" to rule a topic out.
